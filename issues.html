Questions the Government Must Answer

We don't do empty slogans. We ask the questions that make people uncomfortable.

💼 Unemployment & Economy

▼
  • 42% youth unemployment — while politicians' children get plum posts. Where are the 8 crore jobs promised in 2014?
  • 1000+ applicants fighting for a single peon position — while crony capitalists get loan waivers worth lakhs of crores.
  • Recruitment exam paper leaks (UPPSC, NEET, SSC, Railway) — why are lakhs of students' futures destroyed with zero accountability?
  • Where is the employment data? — Why did the government stop publishing reliable unemployment statistics?
  • Why are contract jobs the "solution"? — When will permanent employment be treated as a right, not a privilege?
  • Startup India, what happened? — Where are the jobs that were supposed to come from the startup ecosystem?
  • Why is the informal sector ignored? — 90% of India's workforce is informal. Where's the policy for them?
  • Brain drain is at an all-time high — Why are our best minds leaving and never coming back?

🔍 Anti-Corruption

▼
  • Electoral bonds — Who bought who? Why is the data still shrouded in secrecy?
  • Why no public database of politician assets updated in real-time? If you're clean, why hide it?
  • 80%+ of convicted MPs/MLAs still hold office — how is this legal, and why does nobody care?
  • Where is the Lokpal? — Promised 10+ years ago. Where are the prosecutions?
  • ₹10 lakh crore+ in bank frauds (Nirav Modi, Vijay Mallya, etc.) — why zero top-level accountability?
  • Why do government employees demand bribes for basic services with no consequences?
  • RTI activists are being targeted — why is the Right to Information treated as a crime?
  • Why are tenders given to cronies? — Where's the transparency in public procurement?

📚 Education & Future

▼
  • India spends ~3% of GDP on education — global average is 4.5%. Why are our children underfunded?
  • Syllabus is 30 years behind — while AI replaces jobs, we're still teaching rote memorization.
  • College degrees are worthless without coaching bribes or connections. What's the point of a degree?
  • Why are coaching centers the real gatekeepers? — NEET, JEE, UPSC — the system is rigged for the rich.
  • Where are the vocational training programs? — Why is skill development treated as an afterthought?
  • Research funding is a joke — Why do our best researchers leave for abroad?

🌐 Digital & Media Freedom

▼
  • Internet freedom keeps dropping every year — why is India becoming a digital authoritarian state?
  • Journalists are in jail for asking questions — since when is journalism a crime?
  • No data protection law despite 800M+ internet users — who's protecting our data?
  • Why are social media posts treated as "anti-national"? — Since when did criticism become treason?
  • Internet shutdowns — Why does the government turn off the internet instead of solving problems?
  • Why is the press controlled? — Where are the independent media voices?

🏛️ Youth & Governance

▼
  • Voting age is 18, but contesting age is 25 — why can we vote but not run for office?
  • 50%+ of Lok Sabha held by politicians aged 55+ — median age of India is 28. Where's the representation?
  • No National Youth Commission with actual power — just a toothless advisory body.
  • Why are youth policies made by people who haven't been young in 40 years?
  • Where is the youth quota in governance? — Why are decision-makers all from the same age bracket?
  • Why is "chronically online" used as an insult? — The internet IS where Gen Z organ, learns, and fights.

Got more questions?
