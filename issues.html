🟠 BJP Corruption (2014–2026)

▼
  • Electoral Bonds Scam (2018–2024) — ₹16,000+ crore in opaque, anonymous bonds. 99%+ went to the ruling party. Corporate donors got policy favours, bailouts, and contracts in return. Supreme Court struck it down as unconstitutional.
  • Adani-Hindenburg & US Bribery Case (2023–2026) — ₹17,000+ crore wiped from Adani stocks after fraud allegations. US DOJ charged Gautam Adani with bribery; SEC settled for $275M. Zero action from Indian regulators.
  • Rafale Deal Overpricing (2015–2018) — ₹41,000+ crore extra paid for 36 jets with zero Transfer of Technology. French media exposed middlemen and stolen negotiation documents. Supreme Court gave clean chit — but French courts launched corruption probe.
  • Bank Fraud Epidemic (2014–2026) — Mallya ₹9,000cr, Nirav Modi ₹13,500cr, Mehul Choksi ₹13,500cr, Sandesara ₹5,000cr, DHFL ₹14,000cr, PMC Bank ₹4,300cr, Yes Bank ₹3,000cr. Total bank fraud under BJP: ₹10+ lakh crore. Recovery rate: ~15%.
  • The "Washing Machine" — Opposition Leaders Join BJP, Cases Vanish (2014–2024) — 25 opposition leaders facing corruption probes joined BJP. 23 got immediate reprieve — cases closed or stalled. ED/CBI action: 95% against opposition, 5% against ruling party.
  • Electoral Bonds Quid Pro Quo — Bharti Airtel donated ₹236cr → got spectrum policy favour. Aditya Birla donated ₹285cr → got Vi debt-to-equity conversion worth ₹16,000cr. Future Gaming donated ₹510cr → no action on GST evasion.
  • NEET & UGC-NET Paper Leaks (2024–2025) — Lakhs of students' futures destroyed. NEET-UG 2024 leak forced Supreme Court intervention. UGC-NET cancelled after leak. Zero senior BJP leader held accountable.
  • AgustaWestland VVIP Chopper Scam — Probe Stalled — ₹3,600cr scam. Middlemen extradited, but Indian probes into alleged BJP beneficiaries mysteriously slowed down. Italian court acquitted original accused; CBI silent on new leads.
  • Coal Allocation Scam 2.0 (2015–2026) — Modi govt's own coal auctions benefitted crony capitalists. Two BJP MPs wrote letters warning of "scams and unfair profiteering." Warnings ignored. CBI later found irregularities — no high-level action.
  • Vyapam Scam (Madhya Pradesh, 2013–2025) — Massive admission & recruitment scam under BJP rule. Over 2,000 arrests, 50+ deaths under mysterious circumstances. CM Shivraj Singh Chouhan — no action.
  • Maharashtra Irrigation Scam (BJP-ruled, 2015–2022) — ₹70,000+ crore scam in irrigation projects. BJP ministers including Eknath Khadse implicated. Zero convictions of senior BJP leaders.
  • Jay Shah's Wealth Surge (2014–2023) — Amit Shah's son went from ₹50 lakh turnover company to ₹8,000 crore in 7 years. Contracts linked to Gujarat government. No investigation ordered.
  • Government Advertising Loot (2014–2025) — ₹7,000+ crore spent on self-promotion advertising. Bureau of Outreach & Communication became a propaganda machine. No third-party audit.
  • COVID-19 Procurement Scams (2020–2021) — Overpriced PPE kits, ventilators, and Remdesivir. State govts (UP, Gujarat, MP, Bihar) handed contracts to crony firms. No CBI probe into BJP states.
  • Uttar Pradesh Sand & Mining Scam (2017–2026) — Illegal mining worth ₹10,000+ crore. BJP leaders and mafia nexus exposed in multiple sting ops. Mining mafia with BJP links — minimal action.
  • PSU Disinvestment Fire Sale — Air India, BPCL, BEML, LIC, IDBI Bank sold at alleged undervaluation. Public assets transferred to private cronies. No parliamentary scrutiny of pricing.
  • Railway Contracts & Station Redevelopment Scams — PPP model benefitted select corporates. No transparency in ₹1 lakh+ crore station redevelopment bids.
  • Recruitment Paper Leaks (2014–2025) — UPPSC, Bihar BPSC, Rajasthan REET, Haryana HSSC, Jharkhand JPSC — dozens of exam leaks affecting 10+ lakh candidates. Zero systemic reform.

🧠 What We Must Do — A Vision for India

▼
  • 🔥 Anti-Corruption: Make corruption economically suicidal — Real-time public asset database for all politicians (updated yearly). AI-based tender allocation — zero human discretion. Whistleblower gets 10% of every recovered rupee. Body cameras mandatory at every government service counter. 48-hour resolution helpline for bribe demands with same-day FIR.
  • 💼 Economy & Jobs: Stop begging for MNCs, build our own — ₹1 lakh crore National Startup Fund with zero collateral loans for under-30s. 4-day work week pilot in IT and services. Freelancer protection act — gig workers get minimum wage, insurance, and pension. Manufacturing zones with Vietnam-level incentives, not red tape.
  • 📖 Education: Burn the 1980s syllabus — Scrap rote exams for project-based assessment by 2027. AI, coding, and financial literacy mandatory from class 6. Free vocational training after class 10 (Germany dual-system model). Student loan forgiveness for anyone working in social sector for 5 years. 100% research funding increase — stop our brains from leaving.
  • ⚕️ Healthcare: Health is not a business — Universal healthcare at ₹500/year per family covering everything. 2 lakh new Jan Aushadhi kendras with free generic medicines. Telemedicine hub in every village. Mental health helpline and counselling in every school and college.
  • 🏛️ Governance: Make democracy young again — Lower contesting age to 21. 33% youth quota (under-35) in Lok Sabha and all state assemblies. Mandatory criminal background check before every election with automatic disqualification for serious charges. All MLAs/MPs must publish monthly attendance and work reports.
  • 🌍 Climate & Infrastructure: Treat it like a war — 100% EV public transport in all cities by 2030. Free public WiFi at every bus stop and railway station. Solar panels on every government building. River cleaning authority with actual budget, not just press releases. Afforestation paid as a job — plant ₹100/day per tree planted in degraded forest land.
  • 🛡️ Digital Rights: Your phone is not a crime scene — Strong data protection law with jail time for violations. Zero internet shutdowns except in actual war. Legal protection for journalists — no jail for reporting. Algorithmic accountability for all government AI systems. Right to explanation for any automated decision.
  • 🚇 Urban & Rural: Fix both, not one — ₹50 lakh crore 10-year urban infrastructure plan (metros, water, sewage, housing). Rent control reform to unlock vacant housing. Rural broadband as a fundamental right. Every village gets a weekly mobile health clinic and a monthly job fair.
  • ⚖️ Justice: 2-year case limit — No case in any court takes more than 2 years. Fast-track courts for corruption, sexual violence, and economic offences. 5,000 new judge appointments. FIR must be filed within 24 hours — zero discretion to turn away complainants. Prison reform: decriminalise minor offences, focus on rehabilitation.
  • 💡 Innovation: India's decade starts now — National Quantum Mission with ₹1 lakh crore. Space tech, biotech, and AI — unrestricted research with zero bureaucratic approval for grants under ₹10 crore. Student entrepreneur visa — stay in India and build, don't flee to Silicon Valley. Patent filing subsidised 90% for individual inventors.
