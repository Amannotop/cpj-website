Quick Questions?

Is this a real political party? ▼

Not yet. We started as a satirical movement born from frustration. But the energy is real, the questions are real, and the people behind it are real. Where it goes next depends on all of us.

How can I contribute without joining? ▼

Share our posts. Tag us in your stories. Send us tips. Every share is a vote for accountability. You don't need a membership card to be part of this.

Will my DM be anonymous? ▼

If you send us a tip or evidence, we'll keep your identity private unless you say otherwise. We protect our people.

Who runs this? ▼

A distributed network of Gen Z youth across India. No single leader. No party dues. No hidden agenda. Just a swarm of people who are tired of being ignored.
