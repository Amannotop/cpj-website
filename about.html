About the Movement

How an insult became India's fastest-growing Gen Z movement.

The Origin

It started with a courtroom remark.

On May 15, 2026, during a Supreme Court hearing, the Chief Justice of India reportedly compared unemployed youth to "cockroaches" and "parasites of society" — saying they become journalists, social media activists, and RTI filers to "attack the system."

Instead of getting offended, the internet did what Gen Z does best: it flipped the narrative. A satirical political party was launched the very next day — the Cockroach Janta Party.

Within 48 hours, it had 40,000+ registered members. Within a week, 8 million Instagram followers. MPs offered to join. The movement spread across Bihar, UP, MP, Maharashtra, and beyond.

We're not just a joke. We're a mirror. And the reflection is uncomfortable.

The Mission

What we want is simple.

We want a government that answers to its youth. Not with press releases. Not with deflections. With actual answers.

We ask the questions that make people uncomfortable. We document the gaps between promises and reality. We use satire, memes, and relentless digital pressure to force accountability.

This isn't about being anti-establishment. It's about being pro-truth.

The cockroach survives because it adapts. So do we.

Core Values

What We Stand For

🔍 Anti-Corruption

Real-time transparency in government spending. Public databases of politician assets. Zero tolerance for fraud.

✊ Youth Rights

Employment accountability. Education reform. A National Youth Commission with actual power, not just a hashtag.

📢 Transparency

Open data, open records, open governance. If it's public money, it should be public information.

⚖️ Accountability

Convicted officials shouldn't hold office. Paper leaks should be investigated. Promises should have deadlines.

Still just a meme?

Maybe. Or maybe it's the beginning of something real.
